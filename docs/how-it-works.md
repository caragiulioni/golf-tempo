# How it works

The words on the How it works screen (menu, top item), added in V2-20. The text is by the developer. In the app it lives in `src/app/howItWorksText.ts`; edit it there and keep this file in step.

Headings are small grey capitals, the same style as the "Game length" label. The only bold in the text is "Repetition builds rhythm. Rhythm builds consistency." There is no italic. The screen title is "How it works", in the title size.

## Sequence, tempo & your swing

Every swing has a sequence and a rhythm. Whether you're playing Long Game or Short Game, the backswing takes longer than the downswing. Swing Sequence turns that timing into audio cues so you can hear, feel and repeat the movement.

**Repetition builds rhythm. Rhythm builds consistency.**

The first cue marks your takeaway and the last marks impact. In between, you'll hear your weight shift, then the top of your backswing. Turn on the background loop to help you settle into the rhythm of your tempo or toggle off the weight shift or top to focus solely on motion.

## Long game

Gameplay is for full swings, from rapid to smooth. A faster tempo doesn't mean a better swing. Explore the different options and find the rhythm that feels natural to you.

Practice Drills slow things down so you can focus on each part of the sequence. Take one swing, reset, then follow the 4-3-2-1 countdown that leads into your next takeaway.

## Short game

The same principle, with a shorter swing sequence designed for chips, pitches and putts. Weight shift is more subtle in the short game, so you can turn its audio cue on or off at any time using the Weight shift button.

## Configure weight shift

We estimate that the weight shift takes place at 3/4 of the way through the backswing but golfers may vary. Some may shift their weight slightly earlier or later in their swing sequence. Open Configure weight shift at the bottom of the screen to adjust the timing of the shift cue until it matches your natural movement.

## Notes on the wording

- The developer's original said "Open Configure Weight Shift in the current card". The Configure weight shift panel moved to the bottom of the main screen in V2-09, so the app says "at the bottom of the screen".
- Names of controls match the screen: the "Weight shift" button and the "Configure weight shift" panel (the original had capitals: "Weight Shift", "Configure Weight Shift", "the Shift cue"). Elsewhere it is plain "weight shift".
- "Repetition builds rhythm. Rhythm builds consistency." is its own paragraph, with space above and below, so it stands out.
- "From quick to relaxed" became "from rapid to smooth", to match the RAPID and SMOOTH words on the Tempo slider.
- Bold on Gameplay, Practice Drills and "A faster tempo doesn't mean a better swing.", and the italic sentence, were removed in review. Only the "Repetition builds rhythm" sentence is bold.
- The original had notes in brackets (heading style, bold, italics). They were turned into the styles above and are not shown.
