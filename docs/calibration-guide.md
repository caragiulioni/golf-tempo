# Finding your weight shift position (calibration guide)

For Mike. Take your time, and you can stop and come back later: **APPLY** saves what you have so far. Everything here is also in the app: on the main screen, scroll to the bottom and open **Configure weight shift**, then tap **How to**.

## What you are doing

The weight shift beep (the short, higher one) sits somewhere between the takeaway beep and the top-of-swing beep. You are finding the spot that matches the moment your weight starts moving in your own swing. The app saves what you pick and plays it at every tempo. Long game and Short game are set separately.

## Before you start

- **Use one phone, and the same browser every time.** Your own phone is best. Your choice is saved on that phone and in that browser only. A different phone or browser starts from scratch.
- **Do not use a private or incognito tab**, and do not clear your browser history or "site data" while you are working. Either one erases your saved choice.
- **Use the app often enough.** On an iPhone, Safari can delete a website's saved data if you have not touched that site for about a week of Safari use. Android Chrome does not do this, but clearing site data does the same thing. So **do not rely on the saved value alone: send me the saved numbers as soon as you are happy** (see "Sending it back").
- **Sound:** turn the volume up. On an iPhone, check the silent switch on the side is not on.
- **Screen:** the app keeps the screen awake while it plays. If the phone locks, the sound stops. Open the app and press play again.

## Steps

1. Open the app. At the very bottom of the main screen, tap **Configure weight shift** (the label with an arrow). Tap **How to** if you want the short reminder on screen.
2. Pick **Long game** or **Short game** above. Do one at a time.
3. Pick a tempo with the **Tempo** slider. Start with the one you swing at most.
4. Scroll up and press the big **play** button in the Current session card. You will hear takeaway, the weight shift beep, top, and impact, then a pause, then it repeats.
5. Move the **slider** left or right, or tap **−** and **+** for tiny steps. Left is closer to the takeaway beep, right is closer to the top beep. The diamond on the picture moves with it. When you let go, the sound restarts from the beginning so you can hear the new spot straight away.
6. Keep going until the weight shift beep lands where your weight actually starts to move. Take your time and swing slowly along with it.
7. Press **APPLY** to save it. The line under the slider changes to "Saved: …". If it says **NOT APPLIED YET**, the new spot is not saved.
8. **Check other tempos** for the same game length. The position stays at the same relative spot at every tempo, so it should feel right at each one. If one tempo feels off, change it and press APPLY again, then re-check the others.
9. Change to the other game length and repeat from step 3.

**RESET** puts the slider back to the starting value (75 % for Long game and Short game). It does not save by itself. Press APPLY if you want to keep it.

If you close the panel, or switch between Long game and Short game, with changes you have not applied, the app asks "You have changes you haven't applied". **Keep editing** stays where you are. **Discard** throws the changes away.

## Loop on or off

There is a **Background loop** button under the Current session card. It starts off every time you open the app. The drums can pull your ear toward them, because the weight shift beep never lands exactly on a drum hit. Try both, and **tell me which one you used** when you send your answer. If you get a different answer with the loop on and off, tell me that too.

## Sending it back
1. Open **Configure weight shift** and look at the line under the slider: **Saved: 75 %** (your number). Do this for **Long game** and for **Short game**.
2. Send me both numbers in a message. Only the **saved** number counts. If the line says **NOT APPLIED YET**, press APPLY first.
3. Add a line for: which phone and browser you used, whether the loop was on or off, and anything that felt wrong (a tempo where it never felt right, for example).
If a game says "Saved: nothing yet", you never chose a value for it. If the starting value is right, press APPLY once anyway, so it shows as your choice.

## If something goes wrong

- **No sound:** check the volume and the silent switch (iPhone). Tap the play button again.
- **The sound stops by itself:** the phone locked or another app took over. Open the app and press play.
- **Values look like they reset:** you may be in a different browser or tab type (private), or site data was cleared. Set it up again and send me the saved numbers as soon as you are happy.
- **Something looks broken:** tell me what you pressed and what you saw. I may ask you to open **Audio lab (testing)** in the menu and tap **Copy log** (if the menu no longer has it, I will tell you another way). You do not need to do that unless I ask.
