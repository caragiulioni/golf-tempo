# Finding your weight shift position (calibration guide)

For Mike. About 10 minutes per tempo, longer if you want to be thorough. Everything here is also in the app: open the menu, tap **Config**, then tap **How to**.

## What you are doing

The weight shift beep (the short, higher one) sits somewhere between the takeaway beep and the top-of-swing beep. You are finding the spot that matches the moment your weight starts moving in your own swing. The app saves what you pick and plays it at every tempo. Long game and Short game are set separately.

## Before you start

- **Use one phone, and the same browser every time.** Your own phone is best. Your choice is saved on that phone and in that browser only. A different phone or browser starts from scratch.
- **Do not use a private or incognito tab**, and do not clear your browser history or "site data" while you are working. Either one erases your saved choice.
- **Use the app often enough.** On an iPhone, Safari can delete a website's saved data if you have not touched that site for about a week of Safari use. Android Chrome does not do this, but clearing site data does the same thing. So **do not rely on the saved value alone: press EXPORT and send it as soon as you are happy** (see "Sending it back").
- **Sound:** turn the volume up. On an iPhone, check the silent switch on the side is not on.
- **Screen:** the app keeps the screen awake while it plays. If the phone locks, the sound stops. Open the app and press play again.

## Steps

1. Open the app and tap the **menu button** (three lines, top right). Tap **Config**. Tap **How to** if you want the short reminder on screen.
2. Pick **Long game** or **Short game**. Do one at a time.
3. Pick a tempo under **Tempo to test**. Start with the one you swing at most.
4. Press the big **play** button. You will hear takeaway, the weight shift beep, top, and impact, then a pause, then it repeats.
5. Move the **slider** left or right, or tap **−** and **+** for tiny steps. Left is closer to the takeaway beep, right is closer to the top beep. The diamond on the picture moves with it. When you let go, the sound restarts from the beginning so you can hear the new spot straight away.
6. Keep going until the weight shift beep lands where your weight actually starts to move. Take your time and swing slowly along with it.
7. Press **APPLY** to save it. The line under the slider changes to "Saved: …". If it says **NOT APPLIED YET**, the new spot is not saved.
8. **Check other tempos** for the same game length. The position stays at the same relative spot at every tempo, so it should feel right at each one. If one tempo feels off, change it and press APPLY again, then re-check the others.
9. Change to the other game length and repeat from step 3.

**RESET** puts the slider back to the starting value (Long game 70 %, Short game 60 %). It does not save by itself. Press APPLY if you want to keep it.

If you try to leave with changes you have not applied, the app asks "Leave without applying?". **No** keeps you here. **Yes** throws the changes away.

## Loop on or off

There is a **Background loop** switch on this screen. It starts off every time you open Config. The drums can pull your ear toward them, because the weight shift beep never lands exactly on a drum hit. Try both, and **tell me which one you used** when you send your answer. If you get a different answer with the loop on and off, tell me that too.

## Sending it back

1. Press **EXPORT**. A box of text appears.
2. Press **Copy**, then paste it into a message to me. If Copy does not work, press and hold the text, choose select all, copy, and paste it.
3. Add a line for: which phone and browser you used, whether the loop was on or off, and anything that felt wrong (a tempo where it never felt right, for example).

EXPORT sends your **saved** values only. If the screen says there are changes you have not applied, press APPLY first, then EXPORT.

If a value shows `"applied": false`, that category is still on the starting value and you never chose it. If the starting value is right, press APPLY once anyway so it shows as your choice.

## If something goes wrong

- **No sound:** check the volume and the silent switch (iPhone). Tap the play button again.
- **The sound stops by itself:** the phone locked or another app took over. Open the app and press play.
- **Values look like they reset:** you may be in a different browser or tab type (private), or site data was cleared. Set it up again and send the export as soon as you are happy.
- **Something looks broken:** tell me what you pressed and what you saw. I may ask you to open **Audio lab (testing)** in the menu and tap **Copy log**. You do not need to do that unless I ask.
