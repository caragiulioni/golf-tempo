## 1. Short Game: where does the weight shift happen?

(Status: sent to the client by email. Update this entry if the email covered something different.)

**Background.** In the Long Game diagram, the swing is 4 equal parts: 3 for the backswing and 1 for the downswing. The weight shift sits halfway through the whole swing. That is two thirds of the way from takeaway to top of swing, and the time from weight shift to top of swing equals the length of the downswing.

The Short Game ratio is 2:1, so the swing is 3 equal parts: 2 for the backswing and 1 for the downswing. The Long Game rules can't all carry over, so there are three possible answers. Times shown are for 24/12 (backswing 0.8 s):

| Option | Where the weight shift lands | Time after takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| A. Halfway through the whole swing | 3/4 of the way to the top | 0.60 s |
| B. Same fraction of the backswing as Long Game | 2/3 of the way to the top | 0.53 s |
| C. One downswing-length before the top, as in Long Game | Halfway to the top | 0.40 s |

**Question.** In the Short Game, does the weight shift happen as early as in the Long Game (halfway through the backswing), or later, closer to the top of the swing? A rough "earlier" or "later" is enough. If you're not sure, that's fine: the slider in the app will let you find it by ear.
